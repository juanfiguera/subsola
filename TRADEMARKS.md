# The Subsola name and logo

The code is yours to use under the [AGPL](LICENSE). The name and logo aren't part of that license:
"Subsola" and the Subsola logo are trademarks of Central Park Labs, LLC. This page says how you can
use them. The aim is simple: anyone who sees "Subsola" should be able to trust it's the project at
subsola.com, or a copy that's honest about what it is.

## Fine without asking

- **Saying what your thing is.** "A fork of Subsola", "built on Subsola", "works with the Subsola
  MCP server", "data from subsola.com". Plain, true statements are always fine.
- **Running your own copy for yourself or your team**, changed or not, under any name. That
  includes the name as it appears in the app's own pages.
- **Keeping the name where the license or the code needs it**: copyright and license notices,
  credits, source code, package names and git history.
- **Writing about Subsola**, in articles, talks, videos, reviews and courses.

## Needs a different name

If you offer a copy to the public (a website, a hosted service, an app, a package, a paid product),
and especially if you changed it:

- Call it something that isn't "Subsola" and doesn't look or sound like it.
- Don't use the Subsola logo or anything that looks like it.
- Don't suggest Central Park Labs runs, checks or endorses it.
- Keep "Subsola" out of your domain names, app names and social handles.

You can still say it's based on Subsola, as above. A line such as "Based on Subsola
(github.com/juanfiguera/subsola). Not affiliated with subsola.com" works well.

This matters more than usual here. Subsola shows people what may be under the street before they
dig, and a copy with stale data or different depth rules that carried the Subsola name could put
someone in danger and would be blamed on the wrong people.
